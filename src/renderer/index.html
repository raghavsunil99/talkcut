        AI tools

        Remove retakes

        Finds earlier takes of lines you said again, keeping the last one. You review them before anything is cut.

        Remove filler words

        Finds um, uh and fillers in your language, and sounds with no word. You review them before anything is cut.

        Shorten pauses

        Finds long pauses; you choose how long counts and how much silence stays.

        Studio Sound

        Removes background noise and evens out your voice.

        Auto-frame for 9:16

        Finds your face and fills a Reels frame around it.

        Speakers

        Label who is speaking. ElevenLabs does this on its own.

        Fix words

        Find and replace mistakes everywhere, and keep a word list for next time.

        Media

        Add videos and photos as

        Click an item to add it at the playhead: videos and photos go on an overlay track, music and sounds on an audio track. Music is turned down automatically while you speak.

        Stock videos and photos come from Pexels, which is free but needs your own API key.
