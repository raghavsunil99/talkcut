      Fix words

      Elements

      Click a style to add text at the playhead. It goes on an overlay track: drag it on the video to place it, drag a corner to size it, and drag its ends on the timeline to set how long it shows.

      Selected text

      Each new line is a line of text. In titles and lower thirds the second line uses the accent colour.

      Media

      Add videos and photos as

      Click an item to add it at the playhead: videos and photos go on an overlay track, music and sounds on an audio track. Music is turned down automatically while you speak.
